# Changelog

## 0.2.3

A notch of the wheel that cannot move the frame, past the end of the hotbar with Wrap off or with the weapon in hand the only one on the bar, no longer puts that weapon away. With PlainScroll on Hotbar this happened after the pause, without any key.

## 0.2.2

Landing on the item you already hold unequips it, as its number key would. A tap on Alt brings it back.

## 0.2.1

The frame ticks as it moves, and a lost pick makes a sound. Both follow the game's sound effects volume and only you hear them.

## 0.2.0

A quick tap on Alt goes back to what you held before.

The item under the frame is named above the hotbar. The same spot says what the wheel does when that changes, such as while building.

`SkipItems` lists items the wheel passes over. Item lists also take the names shown in game.

## 0.1.0

First release.
